# Javi Rubio </br> Software Engineer, Technical Writer and Engineering Leader

> _20 years at the intersection of engineering, product and knowledge management_

With **strong technical foundations** (Linux, networking, security, coding and AI), I've built and shipped software in very different business domains: e-commerce, mobile secure messaging, banking and tax compliance, civic and gov tech, and SaaS.

**I care about UX, CX and DX**. That's why **shared ownership** is key to me.  I'm a documentarian at heart, good at explaining and redesigning complex technical systems (including sociotechnical). Lately, I've been **speaking about AX (Agent eXperience)** too, specifically from a docs perspective.

Known for advocating [#NoMeetings](https://nomeetings.work): healthier remote work, with fewer meetings and better async collaboration. Because the most effective way to change how people behave is to change the system they are in, turning better ways of working into the **path of least resistance**.
